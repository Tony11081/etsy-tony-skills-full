# Wooden Sign — PROCESS Example

## Product distinctions

Product: wall or freestanding wooden sign. Product Form, sign structure, installation, and wood/coating are separate from Design Element, Element Style, Placement, and Production Process.

Unknown until evidence: wood species/engineered board, thickness, coating, indoor/outdoor use, hanging load, size, edge finish, and available laser/UV machine profiles.

## Architecture

| Layer | Examples | Decision impact |
|---|---|---|
| Product | sign panel and hardware | function, load, shipping |
| Material | plywood, solid wood, MDF candidate | must be confirmed; controls char/adhesion/strength |
| Surface/Finish | raw, sealed, painted, stained | process compatibility is finish-specific |
| Element | name, landscape, border, icon | design content |
| Element Style | woodcut, flat folk, line art | process expression |
| Placement | main face, corner, border, back, hanging zone | process access/function |
| Process | laser engraving, UV printing, hand painting, cutting | separate production paths |

## Manufacturing paths

- Laser Engraving: Sample Test Required for grain, coating, contrast, smoke/char, fine details, and safe edge distance.
- UV Printing: Sample Test Required for porosity/coating, white ink, adhesion, texture, and cure.
- Hand Painting: separate manual process, skill, variability, dry/cure time, and QC; likely Separate SKU or Listing.
- Laser Cutting: structural process; test thickness, kerf, bridges, holes, sharp corners, char, load, and assembly.

Do not present these processes as equivalent visual options. They create different files, setup, appearance, durability, cost, and buyer expectations.

## Surface zones

Main Face supports primary layout; Border supports frame or secondary motif; Corners support accents; Back supports hardware/instructions; Hanging Area is a protected functional zone.

## Gate

CONDITIONAL PASS only after choosing one material/finish/process candidate. Outdoor claims, food-safe claims, and load capacity remain blocked until tests.

## Design and option guidance

Keep process and material fixed within one Base SKU. Offer a small tested stain/base-color choice only if contrast and procurement are controlled. Fixed placement reduces alignment errors. Names/dates are inputs. Major size changes that require new cut/hanging/packaging plans are Separate SKUs.

## Sample and QC

Test material/coating, fine line/text, filled area, contrast, smoke/adhesion, fixture repeatability, hardware fit/load, edge finish, warp, cleaning, outdoor exposure if claimed, and packaging corner protection. QC checks grain/coating defects, artwork position, spelling, char/adhesion, scratches, splinters, hardware, structure, and packaging.
