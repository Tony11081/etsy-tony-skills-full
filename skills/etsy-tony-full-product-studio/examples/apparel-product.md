# Apparel Product — PROCESS Example

## Input and evidence

Product: personalized sweatshirt. Fabric composition, weight, color, construction, size range, care label, supplier consistency, and print/embroidery profiles are Unknown.

Laser Engraving is not a default recommendation for apparel. Process selection starts from confirmed fabric and visual language.

## Process screening

| Process | When plausible | Main gate |
|---|---|---|
| Embroidery | stable stitch-friendly fabric and motifs | digitization, stabilizer, hoop, puckering, text, wash |
| DTG | compatible fiber/base and detailed color art | pretreatment, white ink, gamut, hand, cure, wash |
| DTF | compatible fabric and bounded graphic | underbase, edge, hand, press access, adhesion, wash |
| Screen Printing | repeatable runs and separable colors | screens/setup, underbase, halftone, registration, order size |
| Sublimation | compatible polyester/coating and light base | composition, migration, heat deformation, mirror |
| Heat Transfer | compatible substrate and film | seams/buttons/zippers, temperature/time/pressure, stretch, wash |

Compatibility remains Sample Test Required until a real garment and process profile exist.

## Zones

Front center/chest, pocket area, back, sleeve, and label/inside may be possible. Protect seams, zippers, buttons, pockets, stretch zones, and hoop/press access. Placement is not the process.

## Design translation

- Fine watercolor may suit DTG/DTF after print tests but requires translation for embroidery.
- Flat folk shapes may suit embroidery and screen printing with process-specific files.
- Fine script at small chest size is conditional for every process.
- A photorealistic portrait is not a simple embroidery file.

## SKU and options

Garment size and base color may be Variations when inventory is tracked. A process change is normally a Separate SKU/Listing. Names are personalization. Limit thread/print colors to tested, readable choices; do not open every garment color because contrast and underbase rules multiply.

## Sample/QC

Test fabric/process pair, small text/lines, largest fill, long name, contrast, placement, stretch, wash, cracking/puckering, color migration, hand, press/hoop marks, and packaging. QC must match garment SKU, size/color, artwork version, placement, spelling, process-specific defects, and care instructions.
