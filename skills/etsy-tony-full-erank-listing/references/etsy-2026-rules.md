## Search model

Optimize the whole listing: title, tags, category, attributes, description, first image, other images/video, price, shipping, reviews, shop trust, engagement, and conversion. Do not imply that title or tags alone guarantee traffic or rank.

## Title

Use this preferred structure:

`critical objective traits + product noun + necessary specification or differentiator`

- State the item clearly and use the product noun once when natural.
- Put the most important objective traits early for scanability, especially on mobile.
- Keep the title at or below 140 characters.
- Treat about 15 words as a clarity target, not a platform hard limit.
- Do not claim that keyword position alone changes ranking.
- Include occasions, recipients, or holidays only when essential to what the item is.
- Move aspirational gifting language and subjective adjectives to natural description copy.
- Avoid repetition, keyword chains, `Best`, `Perfect`, `Beautiful`, `Amazing`, `Sale`, `Free Shipping`, and `Discount`.
- Never use unsupported materials, mechanisms, methods, certifications, brands, designers, celebrities, or medical claims.

## Tags

- Return exactly 13 distinct English tags and render them on one line with English commas.
- Keep each tag at or below 20 characters.
- Use only English letters, numbers, spaces, apostrophes, and hyphens. Start with a letter or number.
- Prefer accurate multi-word phrases and diverse intent coverage.
- Avoid duplicate phrases, trivial singular/plural variants, misspellings, and repeated keyword families that add no new intent.
- Do not waste a tag on an exact category or attribute phrase when that structured field already provides the same match opportunity; a more specific multi-word phrase may still be useful.
- Do not use an inaccurate high-volume term.
- Map every final tag to `ERANK_MEMBER_VERIFIED`, `ERANK_MEMBER_DERIVED`, `SHOP_STATS_VERIFIED`, or `SEMANTIC_COVERAGE` evidence.

## Description

The first three short lines must answer:

1. What is the product?
2. What objective qualities make it desirable?
3. Where, why, or how will the buyer use it?

Naturally include the primary keyword and no more than a few relevant secondary phrases in the opening. Write these lines specifically for the supplied product and buyer intent. Vary sentence structure, benefit emphasis, and use case naturally; never reuse a stock introduction across listings. Do not copy the title verbatim, paste tags, or write a keyword block.

Always deliver the complete ready-to-paste English description, never only the opening or a shortened sample. Put the same three opening lines at the very start of `full_description` so the delivered copy is internally consistent.

Use these sections for a full description:

- `Why You'll Love It`
- `Product Details`
- `Perfect For`
- `Care Instructions`
- `Shipping & Processing`
- `Important Notes`

Prefix every section heading with one relevant, buyer-friendly icon, for example `✨ Why You'll Love It` or `📦 Shipping & Processing`. Choose icons that fit the section and product, vary them across the description, and keep them out of factual specifications and keyword phrases. Icons are presentation aids, not substitutes for complete copy.

Do not use or paraphrase this generic cake-topper boilerplate as a default opening:

- `Add an elegant finishing touch to your celebratory cake with a custom laser-cut acrylic cake topper.`
- `Perfect for birthdays, weddings, bridal showers, baby showers, graduations, and other special occasions.`

If a cake topper is actually being listed, write a new opening from its confirmed design, material, personalization, finish, size, event fit, and buyer intent instead of recycling these sentences.

Use `[add ...]` placeholders for unknown dimensions, weight capacity, assembly, care, or materials. Do not invent them.

Unless the user provides different values, include:

- `Processing time: 1-3 days`
- `Shipping method: 3-7 days`

## Claim safety

- Use `glider` only when a gliding mechanism is confirmed.
- Use `solid wood` only when solid-wood construction is confirmed.
- Use `handmade`, `handcrafted`, `custom-made`, `orthopedic`, `waterproof`, `leather`, `stainless steel`, `sterling silver`, `pure silver`, `vintage`, or similar claims only with evidence.
- Use `silver-tone` only when the visible finish is confirmed and composition is unknown.
- Do not use protected brands, movie names, celebrity names, designer names, or competitor names for traffic.

## Images and CRO

- Keep the product dominant, color-accurate, and easy to understand in the first image.
- Recommend product-only, alternate angle, back/side, material close-up, scale, dimensions, packaging/assembly, lifestyle, and motion/video evidence when relevant.
- Separate query matching from click-through and conversion. A keyword can earn impressions while a weak first image, price, shipping offer, or unclear product details suppress clicks and orders.
