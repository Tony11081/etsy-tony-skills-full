# 售后文案模板（个性化商品）

仅在用户要求 Message to Buyers、包裹卡、发货消息或期待管理句时加载。模板里的方括号是给运营替换的字段，交付给用户前必须替换成已确认事实或整段删除；不得把方括号留到买家可见文案里。

规则：

- 只写已确认的处理时间、校样流程、承运商和到货范围。
- 可以请求真实评价，不能给好评奖励、不能引导五星、不能用评价换折扣。
- 不在消息里推销、不放站外链接、不引导站外交易。

## Message to Buyers（订单确认自动消息）

目标：确认个性化输入、说明下一步、减少来回消息。

```text
Thank you for your order!

Here's what happens next:
1. We'll review the personalization you entered: [names / date / letters]. If anything looks off, we'll message you before we start.
2. [Proof step, only if confirmed: You'll receive a digital proof within 1 business day. Please reply within 24 hours so we can keep your order on schedule.]
3. Processing takes [1-3 business days], then shipping takes about [X-Y business days] via [carrier].

Need to change your personalization? Reply to this message within [12] hours and we'll update it at no charge.

The names and dates in our listing photos are examples; yours will be made exactly as entered.
```

## 发货后消息（可选）

```text
Your order is on its way! Tracking: [link is in your Etsy order]. Estimated arrival: [date range].

Because this piece is made to order, please check the personalization as soon as it arrives. If anything isn't right, message us first and we'll fix it.
```

## 包裹卡（实体卡片）

正面一句，背面三行；不印 QR 到站外。

```text
Made to order for you.

Care: [only if confirmed, e.g. Wipe with a soft dry cloth; keep away from direct heat.]
Questions or a problem with your order? Message us on Etsy first — we'll make it right.
If you love it, an honest review helps our small shop.
```

## 期待管理句库

放描述 `Important Notes` 或 `Personalization` 段，只选与商品匹配且事实已确认的句子：

- `Names and dates shown are examples; your piece will be made with your text.`
- `Colors may vary slightly between screens and the finished item.`
- `Each piece is finished to order; small variations are part of the process.`（仅当确有工艺差异）
- `Size: [X] cm — about the width of a [everyday object] for reference.`
- `Personalized items can only be returned if they arrive damaged or with an error on our part.`（需与 listing 退换政策一致）
- `Please double-check spelling and dates before ordering; we make exactly what you enter.`
- `For US orders, duties are included at checkout.`（仅确认 DDP 后）
