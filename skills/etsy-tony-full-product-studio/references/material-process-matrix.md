# Material–Process Compatibility

Create a row for every proposed material/process pair:

| Material | Process | Compatibility | Reason | Test Required |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Compatibility is exactly: Recommended, Conditionally Available, Sample Test Required, Not Recommended, or Unknown.

## Preliminary guidance

These are screening prompts, not production approvals:

| Material | Process | Preliminary status | Main checks |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stainless steel or coated metal | Laser marking/engraving | Sample Test Required | alloy/coating, contrast, depth, food-contact zone, corrosion, fixture |
| Cotton woven/knit | Embroidery | Conditionally Available | weight/stretch, stabilizer, hoop, density, puckering, wash |
| Cotton or blends | DTF/DTG | Sample Test Required | composition, pretreatment/press, hand, adhesion, wash |
| Polyester | Sublimation | Conditionally Available | polyester content/coating, light base, migration, heat deformation |
| Acrylic | Laser engraving/cutting | Sample Test Required | acrylic type, fumes/process safety, edge, stress cracking, film |
| Acrylic | UV printing | Sample Test Required | adhesion, white ink, varnish, reverse print, surface level |
| Leather or coated substitute | Laser engraving | Sample Test Required | exact material, fumes, scorching, odor, finish, color contrast |
| Wood | Laser engraving/cutting | Sample Test Required | species, grain, coating, char, thickness, structure |
| Wood | UV printing | Sample Test Required | coating, porosity, adhesion, white ink, texture |

## Rules

- A generic material name never proves compatibility. Record grade, coating, finish, supplier, and lot when known.
- Separate substrate compatibility from surface-finish compatibility.
- If safety, fumes, food/skin contact, flammability, or outdoor durability is unknown, the gate is conditional or failed.
- Only dated equipment/sample records may change Sample Test Required to Recommended or Approved for Production.
