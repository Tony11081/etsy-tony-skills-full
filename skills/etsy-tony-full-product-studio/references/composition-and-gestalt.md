# Composition and Gestalt

## Composition choices

Centered, Symmetrical, Asymmetrical, Vertical, Horizontal, Circular, Arch, Border, Corner Decoration, Top-heavy, Bottom-heavy, Diagonal, Scattered, Full Bleed, Badge, Emblem, Large Negative Space, and Dense Decorative.

Select composition from product geometry, safe artwork area, main viewing orientation, text length, motif behavior, process, and visual center of gravity. Do not inherit center placement merely because a competitor uses it.

## Gestalt tools

- Proximity: group names and date as one unit.
- Similarity: repeat compatible stroke weights or motif shapes.
- Continuity: guide the eye from motif to text without crossing a functional zone.
- Closure: imply a frame without crowding edges.
- Figure-Ground: preserve clear readable separation.
- Common Region: use a frame or spatial zone to group related information.

State which principles are active and what buyer reading order they create.

## Balance audit

Record symmetry/asymmetry/radial structure, visual weights, center of gravity, edge tension, directional flow, and orientation. Rotate or view the physical Product in its real use position; a balanced mockup may become awkward in hand or on a wall.

## Negative space

Name areas intentionally left blank, motif-to-edge clearance, text safety, and elements removed. Dense decoration is allowed only when it is the family strategy and readability/function still pass.

## Variable text

Create layout states rather than uniform scaling:

- short: preserve designed width and spacing;
- medium: standard layout;
- long: switch font/line break/secondary layout;
- over limit: proof or reject.

Never shrink text below an untested process threshold to force it into one composition.
