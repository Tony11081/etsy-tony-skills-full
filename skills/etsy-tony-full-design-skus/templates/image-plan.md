## CRO and evidence audit

- [ ] Hero shows the real Product and primary value at thumbnail scale.
- [ ] Finished-item claims are supported by real sample images.
- [ ] Options shown exactly match Active codes and dependencies.
- [ ] Text is readable on mobile and does not obscure the Product.
- [ ] Scale, color, reflection, texture, process result, and included pieces are accurate.
- [ ] No unsupported reviews, badges, scarcity, delivery, guarantee, or comparison claims.
- [ ] Image wording matches title, description, personalization, and production records.
- [ ] Every major buyer concern has one clear visual answer.
