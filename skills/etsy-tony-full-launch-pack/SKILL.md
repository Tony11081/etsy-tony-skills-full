# Etsy Listing Launch Pack Builder

## 当前任务与交付

- 材料：可行的 SKU、工艺状态、真实选项矩阵、商品事实和未知项。
- 范围：资料生成不等于实物或上架就绪；缺失的事实只阻塞依赖它的字段与就绪结论。
- 验收：英文文案、恰好 13 个英文逗号分隔标签、选项和图片计划一致；所有就绪结论有证据。


Require a viable SKU, manufacturing status, real option matrix, codes, and unknown list. Do not compensate for missing product facts with SEO language.

## Runtime verification

Before finalizing, check current official Etsy sources for title, tag, variation, custom-option, image, and personalization requirements. Record source URLs and verification date. If verification fails, write Platform Rule Verification Required and avoid unconfirmed limits.

Also verify that ships-from information reflects the real dispatch point, required production partners are disclosed, delivery estimates match the actual route, and tracking is not selected to hide origin. Do not derive a shipping postal code from an operating IP. For US-bound orders, verify the current Etsy import-duty and Delivery Duty Paid requirements before promising a route.

## Build

- Natural US-English title with Product first and only confirmed differentiators.
- One-line positioning, benefits, use, Gift For, Occasion, Materials, and truthful care/process notes.
- Clear description, personalization instructions, variations, custom fields, attributes, and How to Order.
- Exactly 13 accurate English tags separated by English commas under the user's standing rule, subject to current official rules.
- Image plan selected from hero, close-up, personalization, pattern/font/color charts, size/placement, material/process, lifestyle, package, care, and ordering.
- Each image states purpose, visible evidence, focus, proposed copy, codes, measurements, linked option, and disclaimer.

For personalized commercial products, the primary image needs a real finished personalized item or current official Etsy-compliant evidence; a blank item or placeholder-text mockup is not proof. Label pre-production visuals.

## Consistency gate

Cross-check images, title, descriptions, tags, materials, process, dimensions, P/F/C codes, available options, personalization collection, props, and exclusions. The buyer must be able to order without guessing, and production must receive all required information.

Cross-check the stated ships-from location, actual dispatch point, production-partner disclosure, customs/duty handling, carrier path, tracking behavior, and delivery window. Any mismatch is Fulfillment Disclosure Required and blocks launch readiness.

Return a draft and employee upload checklist. Do not publish without explicit authorization.

完成本轮所需交付及上述验收后结束。内部必要依赖可以继续；不自动追加下一项业务或要求用户重新调用同一任务。
