# Advanced IP Search

Use in FULL-C before final recommendation or launch when actual names, phrases, artwork, fonts, stock assets, packaging, and competitor evidence are available. This extends `originality-and-ip-rules.md`; it is not legal advice or clearance.

## Search scope

Screen separately:

- brand/shop/product/collection/Family and buyer-facing option names;
- slogans, phrases, quotations, lyrics, and copied Listing language;
- logos, characters, recognizable artwork, photographs, patterns, and stock assets;
- font/typeface software and commercial-use license;
- supplier-provided artwork, molds, dies, files, and alleged public-domain sources;
- competitor-specific composition, personalization structure, packaging, and trade-dress-like overall impression;
- design patent/registered design or utility patent questions when Product form/function warrants specialist review.

## Evidence-first search record

For every flagged item record:

- exact term/image/feature and intended use;
- source and evidence classification;
- jurisdiction and market;
- relevant product/service category or classification;
- official database/registry or authoritative license source;
- exact query, image search method, filters, and search date;
- live/pending/expired/cancelled/unknown status as reported by the source;
- owner/applicant, relevant goods/services, result link/reference;
- similarity reasoning and uncertainty;
- required redesign, license, legal review, or rejection action;
- reviewer and decision date.

Use current official sources at execution time. Search-engine results, marketplace presence, AI memory, and absence of a result are not clearance.

## Artwork and asset provenance

Every externally sourced asset needs:

- creator/source identity;
- original URL/file/reference and acquisition date;
- license text/version and commercial/product/print-on-demand/modification limits;
- proof of purchase/subscription where applicable;
- attribution requirement;
- allowed products, territories, quantities, and transfer/sublicense restrictions;
- modification record and final-file linkage.

“Royalty-free,” “free,” “vintage,” “public domain,” or “AI-generated” is not enough without source-specific evidence. AI generation does not prove non-infringement or exclusive rights.

## Decision labels

- No Specific Flag Observed — search found no specific issue in the checked scope; not clearance.
- IP Verification Required — evidence/search is incomplete.
- License Evidence Required — use may be acceptable but rights are undocumented.
- Potential Trademark Conflict — name/mark similarity requires redesign or qualified review.
- Potential Copyright Conflict — art/text/photo/expression requires redesign, license, or qualified review.
- Potential Design/Patent Issue — Product form/function requires qualified review.
- Reject — Protected/Distinctive Expression — the concept depends on material that should not be used.
- Cleared by Qualified Counsel for Defined Scope — use only when a dated legal record states the exact scope.

## Escalation and redesign

Escalate when the result affects a core brand/product name, recognizable entertainment/cultural property, competitor-near overall impression, uncertain license, high-volume investment, tooling, or launch commitment. Do not expose unpublished competitor/private files outside their authorized scope.

Redesign must change the meaningful expression, not merely color, flower, font, or one word. After redesign, rerun the search and originality comparison against the new final artwork/name.
