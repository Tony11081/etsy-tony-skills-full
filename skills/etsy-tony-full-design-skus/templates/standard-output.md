# STANDARD Output Contract

Keep the result compact and use this order. Consolidate repeated facts in shared tables, but do not omit a required Gate or SKU-specific risk.

## 1. Input and Evidence

- Mode and supplied sources
- Evidence limits
- Observed / Inferred with confidence / Unknown table
- Evidence Gate result

## 2. Product and Buyer

- Product Name, Category, Function, and Functional/Decorative/Memorial/Gift role
- Target Buyer, Final User, Gift Recipient, Occasion, Usage Scene
- Emotional, Functional, and Personalization value
- Price position and Seasonal/Evergreen
- Premium drivers, decoration-only features, purchase reasons, concern, and risk

## 3. Product Architecture

- Form, components, material, surfaces, functional/decoration zones, main viewing side
- Size, finish, typography, pattern, personalization, assembly, packaging
- Classify each as Fixed, Variable, Customer Selectable, Conditional, Add-on, Separate SKU, Separate Listing, Not Recommended, or Unknown
- Surface-zone suitability and functional/process risk

## 4. Process Exploration

- Compare 3–5 relevant Ideal Processes before reading current capability
- Visual/material/detail/durability/personalization/batch/scale/complexity/cost/labor/lead-time/premium/failure/supplier/file judgments
- Best Overall, Premium, Cost-efficient, Small-batch, Scalable, and Personalization process

## 5. Current Capability

- Confirm that `references/production-profile.md` was read after Ideal Process exploration
- Ideal Process, Current In-house Capability, In-house Alternative, trade-off, Outsource Route, future candidate, required validation
- Keep unrecorded parameters Unknown / To Be Tested / Awaiting Capability Data

## 6. Competitor DNA

- Product Form, material, finish, main/secondary motif, typography, hierarchy, color, Placement, composition, negative space, personalization, process, customer, emotion, hook, purchase reason
- Separate Generic Category Language, Generic Design Style, Competitor-specific Expression, and Potentially Protected Expression

## 7. Two Design Families

- Use `design-family-card.md` fields
- Include Manufacturing and Design Gate results
- Do not generate recommended SKUs from a failed Family

## 8. Six SKUs

- Create three meaningful concepts per Family when quality permits
- Use `[PRODUCT]-[FAMILY]-[DESIGN]-[VERSION]`
- Use `sku-card.md` fields, compressed without losing code, design, process, options, risk, and test information
- Do not treat names/dates as SKUs or processes as Placement

## 9. Gate Summary

One table with Process Compatibility, seven Design scores and average, Option Error Risk, Originality Distance, Production Status, Feasibility, Commercial Potential, Hard-Gate result, and disposition for all six SKUs.

## 10. Top 3 Calculation

Rank only Hard-Gate-qualified candidates:

`Originality Distance × Production Feasibility × Commercial Potential`

Show every candidate's arithmetic. For each Top 3 provide Originality, Feasibility, Commercial, Core Score, Option Risk, Design Average, Production Status, ranking reason, remaining risk, and action. Apply the documented tie-break order; do not use subjective preference.

## 11. Top 3 Concept Board

- Generate only after the Top 3 ranking is locked.
- Show the three ranked SKUs as distinct, readable concepts in one board.
- Provide the saved image path, manifest path, generation prompt, visual-inspection result, and originality-distance check.
- Caption it `PRE-PRODUCTION MOCKUP / UNVERIFIED AGAINST PHYSICAL SAMPLE` until the exact production scope has physical-sample evidence.
- Do not use the board to claim exact material, finish, dimensions, safety, durability, production result, or listing readiness.

## 12. Employee Actions

Separate Design, Production/Supplier, Listing/Operations, and Manager actions. Include blockers and minimum human decisions. End with execution result, verification result, and VERIFIED_SUCCESS / PARTIAL / BLOCKED / FAILED / UNVERIFIED.

## STANDARD exclusions

Do not add a complete QC manual, complete Listing descriptions, complete sample plans for every SKU, twenty SKUs, or unrelated Phase 2 libraries. When a Hard Gate fails, keep the audit evidence and disposition but exclude the SKU from Top 3 and the concept board.
