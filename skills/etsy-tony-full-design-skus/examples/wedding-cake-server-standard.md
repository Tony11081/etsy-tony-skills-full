| Process candidate | Visual / detail | Material / durability | Personalization / batch | Complexity, cost, lead time | Premium / failure / files | Phase 1 result |

| Mechanical diamond-drag / rotary / CNC engraving by qualified supplier | tactile groove and premium physical character; fine text depends on tool/fixture | must verify coating, burrs, corrosion exposure, cleaning, geometry | personalized units feasible; slower/tooling/fixture dependent | setup/labor Medium–High direction; actual cost/time Unknown | Best Premium candidate; risks are burrs, finish tearing, depth inconsistency; vector/toolpath requirements supplier-specific | Conditional candidate; score 3 |
| Electrochemical etching by compatible metal specialist | flat controlled mark; detail can be good with proper mask | conductive substrate/finish and post-process safety must be verified | variable masks possible; batch economics and one-off labor Unknown | chemical handling/supplier dependency; cost/lead Unknown | potential durable contrast/depth; risks are undercut, uneven mark, residue; mask/vector file | Supplier Partnership candidate; score 3 |
| Photo-chemical etching / controlled chemical etch | detailed repeatable decorative field possible | substrate/coating/chemistry and food-contact/cleaning implications require specialist proof | stronger for batch patterns than one-off variable names | specialist setup and lead time; direction Medium–High for personalized one-offs | premium surface potential; risks are undercut, inconsistent depth, chemical control; mask/separation file | Outsource-only batch/premium exploration; score 3 |

Process Compatibility Score 3 rule for all four candidates: the exact metal/finish is Unknown. Required Design Translation is closed vector line art, outlined type, controlled filled areas, and short/long personalization states appropriate to the chosen supplier/process. Sample Test must compare mark/groove completeness, contrast, finish damage, readability, position, cleaning response, and repeatability on the exact blank. Main Failure Risk is material/finish incompatibility causing weak contrast, surface damage, unreadable detail, or inconsistent placement.

Selected direction:

- Best Overall / Small-batch / Scalable / Personalization: substrate-matched precision laser marking or engraving, conditional on actual material/finish and equipment/supplier tests.
- Best Premium: qualified outsourced mechanical diamond/CNC engraving, conditional on burr, depth, finish, cleaning, and fixture validation.
- Best Cost-efficient: laser route **only if** the exact blank and available process pass; no cost claim is made before time/cost inputs.
- Alternative/Supplier route: electrochemical or photo-chemical etching for designs/batches where supplier evidence justifies it.
