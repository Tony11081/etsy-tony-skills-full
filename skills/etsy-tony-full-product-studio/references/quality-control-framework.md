# Quality Control Framework

Create a process-specific checklist plus universal checks.

## Universal

SKU/order codes; correct Product/material/finish/size; approved artwork version; spelling/date/case; P/F/C/L codes; orientation; scale; placement; functional clearance; surface damage; assembly; cleaning; packaging; evidence photos; operator/reviewer; pass/fail and defect disposition.

## Laser

Position, depth/mark strength, uniformity, burn/heat marks, small text, scratches, orientation, proportion, deformation, coating damage, and fixture repeatability.

## Embroidery

Position, thread color, breaks, skipped stitches, floats, puckering, small text, reverse cleanup, stabilizer, long-name layout, trims, tension, and seam/hoop marks.

## Printing and transfer

Color deviation, registration, white edge/underbase, adhesion, press marks, cracking, wash result, contamination, mirror direction, hand, curing, and edge quality.

## Acrylic and UV

White ink, transparency, reverse-print order, scratches, bubbles, adhesion, edge quality, protective-film removal, varnish, dust, and stand/hole fit.

## Wooden/cut structures

Grain/coating behavior, char, splinters, kerf/fit, minimum bridges, warping, hanging hardware, edge finish, and structural load.

## Defect handling

Record defect type, severity, likely cause, rework possibility, rework method, retest, scrap decision, affected batch, and corrective action. Do not call rework “passed” without a second inspection.

Production Approval Status may move to Sample Passed only with recorded acceptance criteria and results; Approved for Production additionally requires manager release for the tested material/process/file scope.
