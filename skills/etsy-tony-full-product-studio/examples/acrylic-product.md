# Acrylic Product — PROCESS Example

## Correct classification

- Product: personalized acrylic name sign.
- Design Element: name, arch, stars, botanical line, or border.
- Element Style: flat vector, line art, Art Deco, or watercolor.
- Placement: front center, reverse face, corner, edge, stand area.
- Production Process: UV Printing, Laser Engraving, or Laser Cutting.

Laser Engraving is never a Placement.

## Unknowns

Acrylic type, thickness, transparency/color, coating, cast/extruded state, maximum size, edge requirement, stand/hole structure, printer/laser profile, white ink/varnish availability, and indoor/outdoor claim.

## Process paths

- UV Printing: Sample Test Required. Decide front print, reverse print, or back print; layer order, white ink, varnish, mirrored artwork, adhesion, and height clearance are part of the file/card.
- Laser Engraving: Sample Test Required for acrylic type, engraving side, contrast, edge stress, cracking, debris, and film.
- Laser Cutting: separate structural process for outline, slots, holes, or stand; test kerf, sharp corners, fit, cracks, edge finish, and load.

Transparent acrylic often needs a deliberate white-ink layer for opacity. Reverse printing must use the correct mirrored order. Neither is inferred from a mockup.

## Zones

Main face or reverse face may carry artwork. Edges, hanging holes, stand slots, and adhesive zones are functional and need clearances. Protective film is a production state, not a finish offered to buyers.

## Gate and options

CONDITIONAL PASS with one chosen acrylic/process path. Fix process and print side within a Base SKU. Major acrylic color/thickness/stand changes may be Separate SKUs. Keep placement fixed. Offer only tested graphic/text colors. Names are personalization inputs.

## Sample/QC

Test adhesion, white opacity, front/reverse orientation, fine text, filled area, scratch resistance, protective-film removal, dust/bubbles, edge and slot fit, stand stability, packaging abrasion, and any outdoor claim. QC checks layer order, color, spelling, scratches, bubbles, edge quality, film removal, hardware, and packaging.
