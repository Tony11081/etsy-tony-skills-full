# Surface Zone Analysis

Map actual physical zones, not generic “front/back” labels. Start from component geometry and use conditions.

## Zone fields

For each zone record:

- component and exact zone name
- plane/curve/flexible/rigid
- dimensions: confirmed, Unknown, or sample measurement required
- main/secondary viewing side
- main motif, secondary motif, text, and personalization suitability
- function, grip, cutting, cleaning, closure, hinge, or load interference
- wear, abrasion, wash, heat, moisture, and visibility
- fixture and positioning difficulty
- process access and orientation risk
- safe-area and edge-distance status

## Category prompts

- Cake Server: Handle, Blade Center, Blade Lower Area, Blade Edge, Back Side. Protect grip, cutting edge, food-contact and scratch-critical zones.
- Pillow Cover: Front Center, Lower Right, Full Front, Back, Zipper Area. Protect zipper, seams, stretch, and wash behavior.
- Wooden Sign: Main Face, Border, Corners, Back, Hanging Area. Protect structure, hanging hardware, coatings, and edges.
- Pennant: Main Body, Upper Edge, Lower Point, Left/Right Margins, Hanging Edge. Protect seam allowances, rod channel, and narrow tip.
- Apparel: Chest, pocket area, back, sleeve, seam/zipper/button areas. Account for drape, stretch, and fixture/hoop access.
- Acrylic: face, reverse face, edge, hole/stand slot, protected-film side. Account for reverse print, white ink, scratching, and height differences.

## Zone decision

Use Recommended, Conditional, or Not Recommended for each element/process combination. A zone that needs special fixtures or manual alignment must state the added labor and sample requirement. Do not open Placement as a buyer option when reliable registration or preview is absent.
