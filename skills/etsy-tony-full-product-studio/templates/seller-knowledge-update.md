# Seller Operations Daily Knowledge Update

本模板用于把每日自动任务的新内容合并为可复用知识。不要记录人名、账号、订单号、凭据、推荐返佣链接或逐条聊天原文。

## Classification

- Evidence Class: Current Official Policy / Official Support Confirmation / Internal Business Rule / Empirical Observation / Historical Snapshot / Unverified / High Risk
- Confidence: High / Medium / Low / Not Applicable
- Time Sensitivity: High / Medium / Low
- Authorization: Read-only / Draft Only / Explicit Authorization Required / Prohibited

## Quality Checklist

- [ ] No names, credentials, account identifiers, recovery codes, referral links, or verbatim chat transcript
- [ ] Reusable claim is separated from one-off context
- [ ] Current official source was checked when the claim is time-sensitive
- [ ] Internal practice is not labeled as Etsy policy
- [ ] Historical metrics retain date and source scope
- [ ] Unsafe or deceptive instructions are quarantined, not operationalized
- [ ] Duplicate or contradictory knowledge was merged or superseded
- [ ] The final record states what employees may and may not do
