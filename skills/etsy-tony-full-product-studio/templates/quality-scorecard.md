## Decision

- Critical dimensions are Customer Need Fit, Semantic Coherence, Style Coherence, Visual Hierarchy, Composition, Negative Space, Typography when text is used, Originality Distance, Material Compatibility, Process Compatibility, Production Feasibility, Customization Clarity, Option Error Risk, and Listing Readiness. An applicable Color dimension is also critical.
- Critical score below 3: Reject or redesign.
- Average below 4.0: not Priority A.
- Unknown or untested material caps Material Compatibility at 3.
- Missing machine/sample evidence caps Process Compatibility and Production Feasibility at 3.
- Missing cost inputs caps Profit Potential at 3 and requires Requires Cost Input.
- Missing confirmed physical facts or real finished-item evidence caps Listing Readiness at 3.
- Incomplete current IP verification caps Originality Distance at 4 even when visual distance passes.
- A score average of 4.0 can justify Recommended for Sampling; it cannot override a readiness ceiling.

Priority: A / B / C / Reject.

Development Readiness: Concept Candidate / Recommended for Sampling / Listing-Ready / Approved for Production.

Production Approval Status: Concept Only / Awaiting Machine Parameters / Sample Required / Sample Passed / Approved for Production / Rejected.
