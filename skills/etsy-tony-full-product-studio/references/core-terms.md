# 产品与定制分类

分类不清或正在定义 Design Family、SKUs、选项、工艺时读取。

下列命令和非链接相对路径均以本 Skill 的目录为基准。

## Definitions that must not be mixed

- Product: the physical item the buyer receives and uses.
- Design Element: what appears, such as a leaf, name, border, icon, or date.
- Element Style: how an element is expressed, such as line art, woodcut, watercolor, or folk art.
- Placement: where artwork or text sits. Laser Engraving, Embroidery, DTF, and UV Printing are processes, never placements.
- Production Process: how a design or structure is made.
- Design Family: a coherent buyer, emotion, visual, material, composition, and process system.
- Base SKU: a stable internal production identity. Customer names and dates are not new SKUs.
- Customer Selectable Option: a controlled buyer choice.
- Personalization Input: buyer-entered content.
- Add-on: extra paid material, process, labor, or packaging.
- Separate SKU: a meaningful material, process, structure, size, cost, or workflow change.
- Separate Listing: a meaningful buyer, aesthetic, occasion, keyword, price-band, or selection-logic change.
