# Listing Launch Pack and Image Planning Rules

Use in FULL-C only after the selected SKU has passed the required design, manufacturing, customization, originality, production-readiness, and commercial gates. This reference produces a draft unless the user separately authorizes publication.

## Evidence lock

Before buyer-facing copy, lock an evidence table for:

- Product identity, exact material/finish/color, dimensions/size, included pieces;
- production process wording, personalization scope, option rules, care/durability/safety claims;
- processing/lead-time inputs, packaging, price and currency;
- sample/approved-product evidence and real finished images;
- supplier/license/IP and current platform-rule checks.

Unknown facts stay out of buyer-facing claims or appear as internal blockers. Do not fill brackets with guesses.

## Listing consistency gate

Title, description, attributes, variations, personalization instructions, price, processing time, images, image text, tags, SKU/configuration codes, packaging, and production card must describe the same offer.

Check:

- no material/process/finish/color/size claim exceeds evidence;
- buyer choices exactly match the Active option register and dependencies;
- Personalization Inputs do not appear as Base SKUs;
- Add-ons, Separate SKUs, and Separate Listings are implemented as designed;
- proof, over-limit, unsupported character, cancellation/change, and care rules are understandable;
- US-market buyer copy uses natural English and USD;
- exactly 13 accurate English tags, separated by English commas, under the user's standing rule;
- current Etsy title, tag, attribute, variation, personalization, image, AI disclosure, handmade/production-partner, and policy requirements are verified against official Etsy sources at execution time.

Static Skill text must not claim that current platform limits are unchanged.

## Buyer-facing copy rules

- Lead with what the physical Product is, who it is for, and the specific emotional/functional value.
- Explain personalization and ordering in the same terms shown in option images.
- Use confirmed measurements/materials/process/care only.
- Avoid unsupported superlatives, medical/safety/eco claims, permanence, exact color promises, and competitor comparisons.
- State what is included and what props are not included.
- Distinguish real finished-item photos, prototypes, digital proofs, mockups, and process visualizations truthfully.

## Image evidence ladder

Prefer:

1. Real finished personalized Product for the exact production scope.
2. Real close-up showing actual material, finish, process result, and imperfections honestly.
3. Real option/size/packaging examples from approved scope.
4. Clearly labeled digital proof or mockup only when necessary and not presented as finished-product evidence.

An attractive mockup cannot close a sample, production, color, texture, durability, or packaging Gate.

## Image-plan roles

Use only the slots needed and verify current platform limits:

- Hero: immediate Product and value recognition.
- Personalization: fields and finished example.
- Option guide: only Active compatible choices.
- Size/scale: confirmed dimensions and use context.
- Material/process close-up: real result and texture/mark quality.
- How to order: short sequential buyer flow.
- Long-name/edge case: tested fallback, not an untested promise.
- Lifestyle/use scene: target occasion without hiding Product.
- Packaging/gift: actual included or Add-on status.
- Care/limitations: accurate, concise, evidence-backed.
- Trust/process: maker/production-partner explanation consistent with current Etsy requirements.

## Image quality and CRO checks

- thumbnail readability and first-focus clarity;
- accurate crop, scale, color, reflection, texture, and edge detail;
- consistent Family art direction without obscuring differences;
- accessible contrast and readable on-image text;
- no unsupported badges, reviews, scarcity, delivery, guarantee, or comparative claims;
- every buyer question maps to one clear image or copy section;
- mobile-order flow remains understandable.

## Release result

- Draft — copy/image plan exists but evidence or approvals remain.
- Conditional — most fields complete; named blockers prevent release.
- Listing-Ready — exact sample-backed facts, files, options, images, pricing, QC, IP, and current platform checks pass.
- Published — use only after authorized write and independent public/admin reread with Listing ID/status/content evidence.

Listing-Ready is not Published.
