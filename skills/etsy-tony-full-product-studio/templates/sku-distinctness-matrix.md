# Sibling SKU Pairwise Distinctness Matrix

Complete every pair within each Design Family before recommendations.

| Family | SKU A | SKU B | Buyer / Occasion | Semantic Focus | Hierarchy | Composition | Motif Role / Treatment | Personalization Structure | Material / Process Identity | Market Position | Only Motif/Font/Color Swap? | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Pass rule

- Every pair differs on at least two meaningful axes.
- At least one difference must be Semantic Focus, Hierarchy, Composition, Personalization Structure, Material/Process Identity, or Market Position.
- Motif species, font, color, or finish-name swaps alone fail.
- Failed pairs are merged, downgraded to P/F/C options, moved to a different justified Design Family, or rejected.

## Family-level check

After pairwise checks, verify common family anchors remain visible: buyer promise, graphic language, type roles, palette logic, composition principles, process, and option rules. Distinctness must not become style incoherence.
