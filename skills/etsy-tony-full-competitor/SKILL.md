# Etsy Competitor DNA Analyzer

## 当前任务与交付

- 材料：竞品链接、页面截图或已提供的商品资料；保留来源与读取时间。
- 范围：回答当前研究问题；店铺总销量、单品评论和单品销量分开记录，不外推成市场规模。
- 验收：每个关键结论能追溯到已打开的来源；事实、推断、Unknown 和不可照搬的表达分开。


Do not generate SKUs.

## Procedure

1. Inventory every supplied source and access date. For inaccessible links, use existing text/images and request screenshots only if the missing view blocks a key fact.
2. Classify claims as Observed Fact, Reasonable Inference with High/Medium/Low confidence, or Unknown.
3. Identify the actual Product before motifs: function, usage, occasion, buyer, recipient, emotional value, functional value, urgency, seasonality, viewing distance, lifespan, price position, purchase concerns, and premium drivers.
4. Compare image, title, description, tags, price, variations, personalization, and review summary. Record contradictions; images do not prove hidden materials or processes.
5. Decompose Product Form, Material, Finish, motif roles, typography, hierarchy, color, placement, composition, negative space, personalization, likely process, customer, emotional promise, market position, visual hook, and purchase reason.
6. Classify each DNA item as Category Convention, Generic Design Language, Competitor-specific Expression, or Potentially Protected Expression.
7. Extract buyer needs and general design grammar into may-learn fields. Put distinctive artwork, arrangement, copy, logo, characters, and branded language into must-not-copy.

## Output

Return Input Summary, Evidence Table, Product Understanding, Buyer and Purchase Motivation, Competitor Listing Consistency, Competitor Design DNA, Design Elements, Unknowns, and Must-Not-Copy. Preserve original Unknown and “< 20” style source values exactly. Do not claim sales, reviews, materials, machine parameters, or IP clearance without evidence.

完成本轮所需交付及上述验收后结束。内部必要依赖可以继续；不自动追加下一项业务或要求用户重新调用同一任务。
