# 产品与定制术语

涉及商品架构、Design Family、SKU 或选项分类时读取。

下列命令和非链接相对路径均以本 Skill 的目录为基准。

## Core terms

- Product: the physical item the customer buys and uses.
- Design Element: what appears in the design, such as a leaf, name, border, or icon.
- Element Style: how an element is expressed, such as line art, watercolor, woodcut, or folk art.
- Placement: where artwork or text sits. A process is never a Placement.
- Production Process: how the structure, mark, decoration, or finish is made.
- Design Family: a coherent buyer, emotional, visual, material, process, composition, and customization system.
- Base SKU: a stable production identity with fixed form, core design, material, and process.
- Customer Selectable Option: a controlled choice presented to the buyer; an Etsy Variation is one possible storefront implementation of such a choice.
- Personalization Input: buyer-entered content such as names, initials, or a date; it normally does not create a new SKU.
- Add-on: paid extra material, process, labor, time, or packaging.
- Separate SKU: a meaningful material, size, structure, process, supplier, cost, lead-time, or quality-control change.
- Separate Listing: a meaningful buyer, scene, style, price band, search intent, or ordering-logic change.
