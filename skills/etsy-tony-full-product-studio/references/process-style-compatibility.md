# Process–Style Compatibility

Create the matrix before Design Families:

| Design Style | Process | Result | Reason | Translation/Test |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Result is exactly: Compatible, Conditionally Compatible, Requires Translation, Sample Test Required, or Not Recommended.

## Translation rules

- Laser Engraving/Marking: favor monochrome, clear contours, purposeful negative space, and moderate detail. Translate watercolor/gradient/complex color to line art, engraving, dither, halftone, or simplified tonal art only if equipment/material tests support it.
- Embroidery: favor readable shapes, stable contours, intentional stitch directions, moderate color changes, and adequate type. Artwork requires Digitization Required; a PNG is not an embroidery file.
- Chain Stitch: use continuous lines, viable turning radius, suitable weight, and stitch-friendly lettering.
- DTF: consider fabric color, white underbase, edges, hand, breathability, press area, fine detail, and wash.
- DTG: consider fiber composition, base color, pretreatment, white ink, gradient/gamut, and wash.
- Sublimation: require compatible polyester/coating and suitable base color; check mirror, heat deformation, migration, and gradient.
- Heat Transfer: check heat tolerance, seams, zippers/buttons, press access, film, stretch, and wash.
- UV Printing: plan white ink, varnish, adhesion, transparent material, front/reverse/back print, height differences, and pretreatment.
- Laser Cutting: protect structural bridges, kerf, corners, holes, loads, edges, and assembly.
- Etching, Marking, Embossing, and Debossing: analyze material, detail, depth, contrast, surface impact, durability, and tooling separately.

## Example judgments

| Design Style | Process | Result | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Minimal Line Art | Laser Engraving | Sample Test Required | language fits, but line and contrast depend on material/machine |
| Watercolor Floral | Laser Engraving | Requires Translation | hue and washes are not directly engraved |
| Watercolor Floral | DTF | Sample Test Required | printable but white underbase, hand, and wash need tests |
| Flat Folk Floral | Embroidery | Conditionally Compatible | shapes translate with digitization and detail reduction |
| Fine Vintage Engraving | Embroidery | Requires Translation | dense hatching must become stitch-safe tonal regions |
| Fine Calligraphy | Small Metal Zone | Sample Test Required | counters/hairlines and surface contrast are unknown |
| Photorealistic Portrait | Embroidery | Requires Translation | needs thread-painting simplification and high complexity |

A translation must name the new production artwork, not merely say “simplify.”
