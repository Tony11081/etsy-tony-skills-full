# Etsy Cake Knife and Cake Server Design System

## Product Constants

- Product: custom stainless steel cake knife and cake server set.
- Production: laser engraving on metal.
- Core markets: US, Canada, UK, Australia, Western Europe.
- Main uses: wedding, engagement, anniversary, birthday, Sweet 16, milestone birthdays, bridal shower, baby shower, housewarming, family celebration, memorial keepsake.
- Positioning: premium personalized gift, wedding keepsake, cake-cutting photo prop, family keepsake, long-term commemorative object.

## Core Design Principles

- The server carries the main story; the knife carries the supporting line.
- Prefer restraint, clear hierarchy, premium whitespace, and legibility over decoration.
- Use simple marks: fine rings, short dividers, small heart, botanical sprig, laurel, star point, ribbon, tiny bow, crown, monogram frame, or oval wreath.
- Avoid cheap clip-art, cartoon feeling, heavy borders, dense florals, hard-to-read over-curled scripts, IP/logo references, and copycat Etsy designs.
- Fonts should feel commercially safe and vector-friendly. Pair a classic serif or high-contrast editorial serif with a clean sans serif. Use script only for short emotional accents such as "Mr. & Mrs." or a name.
- For production notes, mention line thickness, minimum readable text size, safe margins, vector redrawing, and final font license review.

## Fifteen Base Concept Families

1. Modern Monogram Wedding
   - Server: large couple initials or surname monogram, names and date underneath.
   - Knife: wedding date, family surname, or "The Parkers".
   - Best for: core wedding listing, strong personalization diagram.

2. The First Slice
   - Server: "The First Slice", names, date.
   - Knife: "Cutting into Forever".
   - Best for: emotional wedding photo moment.

3. Venue Keepsake
   - Server: names plus venue or city.
   - Knife: date or "Where Forever Began".
   - Best for: destination, winery, garden, beach, or estate weddings.

4. Botanical Vows
   - Server: names framed by minimal botanical line sprigs.
   - Knife: "Forever Starts Here".
   - Best for: garden wedding, bridal shower, feminine premium gift.

5. Classic Mr. & Mrs.
   - Server: "Mr. & Mrs. Thompson" with date.
   - Knife: "Established 2026".
   - Best for: traditional wedding buyers, parents gifting newlyweds.

6. Minimal Name Line
   - Server: names only, plus small date.
   - Knife: numeric date.
   - Best for: modern minimal wedding, low-risk production.

7. Our Forever
   - Server: "Our Forever", names, date.
   - Knife: "Est. 2026".
   - Best for: engagement and anniversary crossover.

8. Engaged Era
   - Server: "Engaged", names, date.
   - Knife: "She Said Yes" or "He Asked. She Said Yes."
   - Best for: engagement party and maid-of-honor gifts.

9. Anniversary Est.
   - Server: anniversary number or "Est. 1996" plus names.
   - Knife: "Still Choosing You".
   - Best for: parents, spouse gifts, high-emotion keepsakes.

10. Sweet 16 Keepsake
   - Server: large "16", name, date.
   - Knife: "Sweet Sixteen", name, or "Make a Wish".
   - Best for: parent to daughter, birthday party gift.

11. Cheers to Thirty
   - Server: large age number with "Cheers to 30" and name.
   - Knife: "A Year to Remember" or date.
   - Best for: 21, 30, 40, 50, 60 milestone variants.

12. Fabulous at 50
   - Server: "Fabulous at 50" with name.
   - Knife: "Celebrating [Name]".
   - Best for: mother, friend, sister milestone birthday.

13. Born to Celebrate
   - Server: "Born to Celebrate", name, birth date.
   - Knife: "Every Birthday After".
   - Best for: baby shower, first birthday, long-term child keepsake.

14. Family Celebration Set
   - Server: family surname or "Smith Family Celebrations".
   - Knife: "Made for Sweet Moments".
   - Best for: housewarming, holiday, all-year family gift.

15. Simple Celebration
   - Server: "Celebrate" plus name.
   - Knife: date or "A Sweet Moment".
   - Best for: all-occasion personalized gift.

## Mockup Prompt Pattern

For real product photos, use a prompt like:

`Edit only the provided product photo. Preserve the product shape, blade shape, handles, cake, flowers, fabric, lighting, shadows, angle, crop, and background exactly. Only replace the existing engraving on the cake server blade and cake knife blade. Make the engraving follow the metal perspective as realistic light gray laser etching. Use the approved design layout exactly: [describe server text, knife text, graphic elements, positions]. Do not add new objects, color, texture, shadows, or background changes.`

For blank outline templates, use a prompt like:

`Edit only the provided black-and-white line-art template. Preserve the exact white background, knife and server outlines, handles, serrations, spacing, and canvas. Remove existing engraving text. Add only crisp black laser-engraving-style typography and tiny line elements inside the utensil surfaces. Keep all text inside the metal shapes with generous margins.`
